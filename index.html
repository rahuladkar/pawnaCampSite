Questions people ask

How do I book?

Send your dates, number of people and package on WhatsApp, or call 95458 73420. Pay 40% to confirm the tent and the rest when you check in.

How do I reach Pawna Lake?

Come by your own vehicle. There is parking at the campsite. If you use public transport, take a train to Lonavala or Kamshet and hire a private jeep to the campsite. We can help you arrange it.

How are the tents arranged?

Each tent is for 2 or 3 people. You never share with strangers. Mattresses, bedsheets, pillows and blankets are provided. We don't provide sleeping bags, for hygiene.

What food do we get?

Evening snacks are tea and pakoda. For barbecue (limited), veg is paneer with vegetables and non-veg is chicken with vegetables. Dinner is unlimited. Veg has sabzi or paneer, chapati, dal, rice, salad and a sweet. Non-veg has chicken or egg curry with chapati and rice. Breakfast next morning is tea, poha, bread and jam.

Is it safe for women and families?

Yes. The campsite has friendly staff, a first aid kit and safe camping for women. Western toilets are available.

What do children pay?

Children above 5 years pay half the package price. For younger children, ask us on WhatsApp.

Can we stay in a villa instead?

At Pawna Lake we offer tents, triangle tents and cottages. If you want a villa, we can suggest options in Lonavala.
